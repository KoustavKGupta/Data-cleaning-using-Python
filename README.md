This is a data of 500 patients of which 350 patients participated in a clinical trial. None of them were using Novodra (a popular injectable insulin) or Auralin (an oral insulin being researched) as a primary source of their insulin. All were experiencing elevated Hba1c level.

All 350 patients were treated with Novodra to establish a baseline Hba1c level or insulin dose. After 4 weeks which is not enough time to observe the change in Hba1c level that can be attributed by the switch to Novodra or Auralin.

175 patients were switched to Auralin for 24 weeks
175 patients were switched to Novodra for 24 weeks
Data for patient experiencing adverse effects is also recorded.
